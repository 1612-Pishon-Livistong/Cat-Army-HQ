About Us

The Mission

We aren't just pets; we are a global feline force. The Cat Army was founded to unite the 600 million to 1 billion cats across the globe—from the smallest house tabby to the mightiest lion. Our goal is to provide a next-generation platform where felines can share intel, master the art of the 3:00 AM zoomies, and finally figure out how to open the tuna cans ourselves.
